## Deterministic movement

Keep MAP-1 input validation-before-mutation, normalized digital direction, speed180, positive TimeSpan including MaxValue, camera derivation, edge-action ignore and single-portal semantics. Resolve a deterministic Y-then-X L-shaped path, not continuous diagonal time-of-impact or physics simulation:

1. Clamp requested Y to world bounds. At old X, only solids whose horizontal interval strictly overlaps the body participate. Sweep from old Y to requested Y: down crossing a solid top stops at solid.Y-32; up crossing a solid bottom stops at solid.Y+Height. Choose nearest blocking contact regardless of list order (algorithmic review only for multi-solid ordering; fixed public fixtures each contain one wall). Edge touch on the perpendicular axis does not block parallel movement. No penetration/tunneling even when an axis displacement passes completely through a wall.
2. Compute requested X from old X. At resolved Y, sweep X against solids with strict vertical overlap: right crossing stops at solid.X-24, left crossing at solid.X+Width, choosing nearest contact. Do not clamp X until after passage evaluation. This preserves MAP-1 resolved-end-Y eligibility. A large diagonal step may travel around a corner via its defined axis path; no claim of straight-line swept2D collision.
3. If collision-resolved X strictly crosses the existing eligible edge passage, transition exactly once to unchanged destination spawn, discard remaining displacement. Otherwise clamp X to world bounds and commit resolved Y. Contact alone does not transition; wall sliding occurs when the unblocked axis advances. End state never strictly overlaps a solid. Invalid update still leaves all state unchanged.

No restitution/velocity/contact report/slope/moving bodies/pathfinding/general collider API. All arithmetic remains finite for existing bounded coordinates and every TimeSpan. Solids and resolution are consumer-owned source content/policy, not mandatory platformer or RPG rules.
