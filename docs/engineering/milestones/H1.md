# H1 — Candidate external platformer validation

Status: candidate, not accepted or implemented. H0 accepted 2026-10-15;
no H1 construction authority yet.

The [roadmap](../roadmap.md) uses a Mario-inspired external game to validate general
capabilities, not a genre-specific Core. Proposed [H1.0](../specs/h1-first-playable.md)
covers only primitive presentation/input/time and consumer-owned movement/jump/floor
rules. It cannot close full H1: animation, content and richer spatial behavior remain
unrefined CAP-005/006/007/008 gaps.

## Preparation evidence

ME-005 scenario and ME-006 [options](../investigations/h1-native-options.md) feed
proposed [ADR-0003](../decisions/0003-next-validation-tracer.md).
[Backlog](../backlog.md) alone owns task states. No native test, visual artifact,
playability result or accepted public contract exists yet.

## Future delivery gate

Before implementation: accepted bounded scope/dependency authority, reviewed exact
packet and ready construction tasks. Technical completion needs automated scenario
traces, H0 regressions and actual Windows deployment/start/stop evidence with limitations.
Owner acceptance evaluates the delivered product slice separately. Refine full-H1
criteria before claiming full-H1 delivery; preserve H0 criteria unchanged.
