## Selection and limitations

- `character.png`: only four walking/idle direction groups, no swords/equipment actions.
- `Overworld.png`: only one grass region and one stone region. No tree regions rendered.
- `Inner.png`: only floor and gate regions. Other furniture is unused.

Publication comments raise an unverified similarity concern about some trees and
Nintendo's Minish Cap; the author disputes that concern. This is not a finding of
infringement. Original complete sheets are retained for hash-verifiable provenance,
including unused pixels; omitting tree draw calls does not remove those pixels from
the distributed source sheet or constitute legal clearance. Reassess before broader
asset use or external distribution. No proprietary Nintendo assets are intentionally
imported. Existing Kenney icons and their separate provenance remain unchanged.
