# Code of Conduct

MicroEngine is made by people who want more games in the world. Whether you are shipping your first game, reporting a
bug or reviewing a pull request, we want every conversation here to be welcoming, useful and kind.

## Our pledge

We pledge to make taking part in MicroEngine a harassment-free experience for everyone, regardless of age, body size,
visible or invisible disability, ethnicity, sex characteristics, gender identity and expression, level of experience,
education, socio-economic status, nationality, personal appearance, race, caste, colour, religion, or sexual identity
and orientation.

## What we expect

- Be welcoming and patient, especially with people who are new to game development, C# or open source.
- Respect different viewpoints and experiences; disagree about ideas, never about people.
- Give and accept constructive feedback gracefully, and assume good intent.
- Focus on what is best for the people who make games with MicroEngine and for the community.
- Take responsibility for mistakes, apologise to those affected, and learn from them.

## What is not acceptable

- Sexualised language or imagery, and sexual attention or advances of any kind.
- Trolling, insulting or derogatory comments, and personal or political attacks.
- Public or private harassment, including continued contact after being asked to stop.
- Publishing someone's private information, such as a physical or email address, without their explicit permission.
- Sustained disruption of discussions, reviews or other collaboration.
- Any other conduct that could reasonably be considered inappropriate in a professional setting.

## Scope

This Code of Conduct applies in every MicroEngine space: the repository, its issues, pull requests and discussions,
the website, and any other channel where someone represents the project in public.

## Reporting

If you experience or witness behaviour that breaks this Code of Conduct, tell the maintainer privately through the
contact options on [their GitHub profile](https://github.com/NastMz), or use GitHub's **Report content** option on the
comment, issue or pull request. Include links to what happened and any context that helps. Every report is reviewed
promptly and fairly, and the privacy and safety of the person reporting are respected.

Security vulnerabilities are not conduct issues: report them as [SECURITY.md](SECURITY.md) describes.

## Enforcement

Maintainers may remove, edit or reject comments, commits, code, issues and other contributions that do not follow this
Code of Conduct, and will explain moderation decisions when appropriate. Depending on the impact and how often it
happened, they respond with:

1. **Correction.** A private, written note explaining why the behaviour was inappropriate, and possibly a request for a
   public apology.
2. **Warning.** A warning with consequences for continued behaviour, and no interaction with the people involved for a
   period of time.
3. **Temporary ban.** No interaction or public communication with the community for a period of time.
4. **Permanent ban.** No public interaction with the community, for a pattern of violations or a single severe one,
   such as harassment of an individual.
